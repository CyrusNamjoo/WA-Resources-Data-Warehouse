# Memo: What drives WA's resource sales, royalties and jobs

**To:** Resources policy / strategy analyst
**From:** Sirous Namjoo, Data Analyst
**Data:** WA Department of Mines resources data files (2025), financial years 2003-04 to 2024-25
**Source view:** `star.v_group_year_summary` (Snowflake)

## Summary

WA's resources sector has moved from a mix led by petroleum to one dominated by iron ore. Iron ore now brings in more than half of all sales and four-fifths of the State's royalties, while gold supports a much larger share of jobs than its share of sales.

## Findings

**1. Iron ore has replaced petroleum as the sector's main source of value.**
In 2003-04, petroleum led with 35% of sales and iron ore had 20%. By 2024-25, iron ore made up **56% of all sales** (A$125.8bn), with petroleum second at 22%.

**2. Iron ore and lithium grew fastest; diamonds have almost disappeared.**
Iron ore sales grew about **23 times** (A$5.4bn → A$125.8bn). Lithium grew from almost nothing (A$0.02bn) to A$4.2bn. Diamond sales fell from A$0.52bn to about A$0.01bn, in line with the closure of the Argyle mine in 2020.

**3. The money and the jobs sit in different places.**

| 2024-25 | Share of sales | Share of mining jobs | Share of royalties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Ore | 56% | 49% | **81%** |
| Gold | 13% | **27%** | 6% |

Iron ore generates **A$1.92m of sales per job**; gold generates A$0.81m. Gold supports a quarter of mining jobs with an eighth of sales, while iron ore funds most of the State's royalty income.

## So what

- **State budget risk sits with iron ore.** A fall in the iron ore price would hit royalty income far more than any other commodity.
- **Jobs risk is more spread out.** Gold, lithium and nickel together hold over a third of mining jobs (37%), so a downturn in those commodities matters more for employment than for royalties.

## Read with care

- **Values are nominal**, not adjusted for inflation. Growth over 20 years is smaller in real terms.
- **Petroleum has no job figures** in the mining employment data, so it's left out of jobs comparisons, not counted as zero.
- **Royalties include North West Shelf grants** (Commonwealth payments for offshore petroleum), matching the source's *Total Royalty Revenue*.
- **Some sales values are confidential** ("nfp"): 68 values across 7 small commodities. In 2024-25 this is A$49.8m (Tin and Tantalum), 0.02% of total sales.
